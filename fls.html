An original creative brand built around bird behavior, ornithological names, language, and irreverent humor. A published coloring book brings its character development and visual storytelling into print; stationery collections remain in development.

Published product

From bird puns to a coloring book.

Work included concept selection, iterative visual review, preparing an 8.5 × 11-inch paperback interior, coordinating a cover, and navigating Amazon KDP publication. Role: concept, naming, writing, creative direction, content selection, review, layout decisions, and independent publishing.

Featured stationery case study · Concept

Rockin’ Robin: Seven Birds, Seven Colorways, One Remix

A seven-member fictional bird band developed into a proposed family of coordinated stationery sets, with a customizable special edition inspired by Weird Owl Yankovic.

A repeatable product system

Seven musicians each have a designated color. Standard sets include a compact journal, coordinating pen, compatible leather pen sleeve, sticky notes, and three matching folders. The Weird Owl edition lets customers mix colors across components and replaces the folders with a Hawaiian-print accordion file. These are functional specifications and approved concept mockups, not manufactured merchandise.

Creative direction: Define the assortment, evaluate inconsistent mockups, reference functional products, and resolve the approved visual directions.
