About

Different formats. One approach.

Built around people and how they think.

I’m an educator and counselor with experience across behavioral health, university instruction, youth programming, and curriculum development. My work brings together learning design, writing, research, and creative problem-solving.

Whether the outcome is a facilitator guide, a comparative reference entry, a published book, or an ensemble narrative, I care about clarity, context, and the details that make an idea work.
