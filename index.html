About the designer

Clinical depth. Educational clarity.

Built for people, not just presentations.

My experience spans behavioral-health treatment, psychoeducational curriculum, university instruction, youth programming, and program operations. I approach learning design by asking what participants need to understand, what they can meaningfully practice, and what a facilitator needs in order to teach responsibly.

Across audiences, my work emphasizes plain language, practical application, evidence-aware content, and respect for learner autonomy.

Credentials: Master of Education (M.Ed.) · National Certified Counselor (NCC) · Mississippi Licensed Professional Counselor / LPC-S.
